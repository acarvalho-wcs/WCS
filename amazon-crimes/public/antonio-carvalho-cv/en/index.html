01 / PROFILE

Science, intelligence and action.

I am a Brazilian biologist and PhD in Evolutionary Genetics and Molecular Biology. My work has evolved from biodiversity, genetics and biogeography to addressing the illegal exploitation of wildlife and other threats to conservation.

At WCS Brazil, I combine open-source intelligence, conservation criminology, spatial analysis, emerging technologies and cross-border collaboration. I also develop analytical systems that help transform dispersed information into contextualized, actionable intelligence.

My work also encompasses scientific communication and technical writing in Portuguese, English and Spanish, documented through publications, reports and outreach materials for different audiences.
