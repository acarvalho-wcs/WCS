01 / PROFILE

Conservation intelligence with scientific depth and operational leadership.

I am a Brazilian biologist with a PhD in Evolutionary Genetics and Molecular Biology and two postdoctoral appointments. I lead the Conservation Intelligence Program at WCS Brasil, combining environmental crime analysis, cross-border cooperation and technology-driven approaches to conservation.

From 2022 to 2026, I helped prepare proposals, secure funding and coordinate the implementation and financial management of international projects with combined budgets of approximately US$6.2 million, working alongside international and multiethnic teams. These results reflect shared responsibility rather than individual fundraising.

I lead engagement and technical dialogue with environmental agencies, law enforcement and intelligence bodies, the Armed Forces, prosecutors, the judiciary and executive ministries. I have led, facilitated or supported meetings and training with authorities across Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Bolivia, Colombia and Peru.

I also create multilingual analytical platforms, including the Global Wildlife Trafficking Observatory and Aurum, using OSINT, geospatial analysis and human–AI workflows to turn dispersed evidence into useful intelligence. My scientific and technical writing spans Portuguese, English and Spanish.
